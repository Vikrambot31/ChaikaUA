# Image Style Guide

Create a consistent visual identity for Telegram posts about Human Design and self-knowledge.

Style:
- calm premium editorial illustration;
- soft natural light;
- modern symbolic composition;
- warm off-white background with accents of teal, coral, graphite, muted gold, and sage;
- clean geometry inspired by charts, gates, energy centers, constellations, and human silhouettes;
- subtle paper texture;
- no readable text inside the image;
- no logos, watermarks, screenshots, UI, or social media frames;
- respectful, grounded, non-esoteric mood.

Composition:
- square image, 1024x1024;
- clear central symbol or human figure;
- enough quiet space for Telegram preview cropping;
- polished and coherent, not cluttered.

Avoid:
- dark fantasy aesthetics;
- neon cyberpunk;
- medical anatomy;
- religious symbols;
- stereotypical astrology imagery;
- photorealistic faces of real people.
